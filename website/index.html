AI Diary

Every night, Atheletia can automatically write a diary entry based on what you actually did that day — apps used, music played, problems solved. Edit it, add your own thoughts, or let it run on autopilot.

A Notion-style markdown editor with an AI assistant built in. Write notes, ask questions about your own documents, and keep everything organized in folders. All stored locally on your machine.

See exactly where your time goes. Tracks which apps you use and for how long, automatically categorizing time into productive, neutral, and distracting. No browser extension needed.

Search and play music from YouTube without opening a browser. Create playlists, control playback from anywhere in the app, and sync music with your focus timer for the perfect work session.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything is stored locally on your computer in a SQLite database. Your activity data, notes, diary entries, and settings never leave your machine unless you explicitly sync them somewhere.

Do I need an AI API key?

For AI features (diary generation, chat, hints), yes. You can use OpenAI, Anthropic, NVIDIA NIM, Google Gemini, Groq, or even local models via Ollama. The app works without AI, but you'll miss the best features.

What does activity tracking actually track?

It tracks which applications you have focused on your screen and for how long. It doesn't record keystrokes, screenshots, or content within apps — just the app name and window title.

Can I turn off tracking?

Yes. You can pause tracking anytime from the dashboard, exclude specific apps, or turn it off entirely in settings. You're always in control.

Does it work offline?

Mostly. Activity tracking, notes, diary, music, and focus timer work offline. AI features need an internet connection to reach your chosen AI provider's API.

Is this free?

Yes, Atheletia is free to use. You only pay for your own AI API usage if you use AI features. There's no subscription or hidden fees.
