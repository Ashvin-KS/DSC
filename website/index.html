Before you buy

What people usually ask first.

Does Atheletia require a cloud account?

No. The desktop app can run locally. AI and Google features only need accounts or keys when you choose to connect those services.

Which AI providers are supported?

NVIDIA NIM, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Gemini, and local LM Studio-compatible models are represented in the app settings.

Is this Windows-only?

Windows is the primary target because several integrations depend on Windows APIs. Other platforms may have reduced behavior.

Where should screenshots go?
